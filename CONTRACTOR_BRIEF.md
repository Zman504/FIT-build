# Contractor Brief — FIT / LifeClock Landing Page

## Objective

Design a polished, high-converting landing page experience for cold paid traffic.

The page should grab attention within 2 seconds and make the Life Score concept
instantly understandable.

## Product

FIT / LifeClock is a nutrition app. Users log a meal and receive a Life Score — a
simple meal-impact score based on real nutrition data, not just calories.

## Primary positioning

Simple meal-impact tracking without diet-app bloat.

## Better consumer framing

Use:
- Life Score
- meal-impact score
- what helped, what hurt, and why
- nutrition awareness
- not just calories

Avoid:
- heavy medical framing
- guilt/restriction language
- implying one meal literally determines lifespan
- making GLP-1 the primary brand angle
- generic diet-app language

## Target users

- people tired of bloated calorie trackers
- people who want a simpler nutrition app
- macro / fitness nutrition users
- high-protein meal prep users
- practical weight-loss maintenance users
- budget wellness users
- nutrition app comparison shoppers
- GLP-1 users are adjacent, not the core brand direction

## Desired pages/components

1. Main public landing page
   - bold hero
   - dynamic meal-card visual
   - clear Life Score explanation
   - strong CTAs
   - mobile-first layout
   - repeated CTA lower on page
   - trust/disclaimer text

2. How-it-works/detail page or section
   - AI helps identify the meal
   - public nutrition data fills in the numbers
   - a consistent scoring system calculates Life Score
   - user sees top positive and negative drivers
   - nutrition awareness only, not medical advice

3. Contact page
   - contact email: mylifeclock@proton.me
   - support
   - creator partnership
   - business inquiry
   - feedback

## Visual direction

The page should feel like a polished consumer app, not a clinic site or generic
SaaS template.

Borrow inspiration from:
- MyFitnessPal for mainstream nutrition-app structure and CTA clarity
- Yazio for friendly brand feel and gamification
- Duolingo for simple, fun, habit-forming consumer-app clarity
- Oura for premium consumer-health polish
- Headspace for approachable wellness tone
- Levels for food-feedback clarity, but less clinical

Do not copy any reference directly.
