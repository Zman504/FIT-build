# Reference Notes

## Direction

The current page is too raw and can feel too medical/longevity-heavy. The new
direction should be more consumer-friendly, gamified, visual, and clear.

## Reference sites

MyFitnessPal:
Use for mainstream nutrition-app structure and CTA clarity.

Yazio:
Use for friendly consumer nutrition branding, gamification, and easy app feel.

Duolingo:
Use for immediate simplicity, habit-forming product energy, and friendly tone.

Oura:
Use for premium consumer-health polish, spacing, and product visualization.

Headspace:
Use for approachable wellness tone and non-intimidating design.

Levels:
Use for food-feedback clarity, but avoid becoming too clinical or medical.

## Creator feedback to account for

Some nutrition creators may react negatively to hard lifespan/minutes claims
because they can feel black-and-white or stressful for audiences dealing with
food anxiety, restriction, metabolic concerns, or GLP-1 use.

So the page should frame Life Score as:
- a meal-impact score
- nutrition awareness
- simple feedback
- not medical advice
- not a literal claim that one meal determines lifespan
